## Always Remember
- NO prices (everything complimentary)
- Dark/gold aesthetic — NOT green/white sport look
- Every text = `data-en` + `data-ru` attributes
- Mobile-first (375px primary)
- General menu ≠ VIP menu — different tiers, different feel
